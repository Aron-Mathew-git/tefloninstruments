03 // HOW IT WORKS

How It Works

Our straightforward calibration process ensures accuracy, unbroken traceability, and audit compliance.

STEP 01

Intake & Logistics

Instruments are received, logged into our secure Cal-Cloud database, and assigned unique metrological tracking IDs for clear custody.

STEP 02

Thermal Acclimation

Equipment is acclimated inside our clean environmental chambers (20°C ±0.5°C, 45% ±5% RH) to eliminate materials expansion skew.

STEP 03

Precision Calibration

Trained metrologists perform comparative multi-point calibration runs against master references traceable to NPL and NIST standards.

STEP 04

Certification Release

Uncertainty budgets are computed. A traceable calibration certificate is signed, sealed, and uploaded to the client portal for instant access.
